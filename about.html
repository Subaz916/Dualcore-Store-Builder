We believe everyone should be able to sell online.

DualCore was born from a simple frustration: launching a store shouldn't require an agency, a developer, or a thousand-dollar budget.

Our story

We started DualCore in 2025 with a small team of designers and engineers who kept meeting store owners stuck on spreadsheets, WhatsApp order forms and templates that broke on mobile.

So we built the platform we wished existed: a drag-and-drop store builder that outputs pure, fast, beautiful HTML — the same quality code a professional agency would ship, generated in minutes.
